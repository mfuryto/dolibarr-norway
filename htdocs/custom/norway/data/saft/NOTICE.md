# Official Norwegian SAF-T reference material

These schema and code-list files are published by Skatteetaten for implementing
Norwegian SAF-T. Original files are preserved. Their upstream paths, revision
and SHA-256 digests are recorded in manifest.json. They are normative reference
material, not accounting records or an assertion of compliant implementation.

The module's GPL applies to its implementation code; this notice does not
relicense the authority's original material. The Regnskap Norge General Ledger
Standard Accounts are not bundled here or used to replace the installed chart.
The grouping list is the authority's 2025–2026 list for næringsspesifikasjon.
